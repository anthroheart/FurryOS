🏗️ The Toolset of Creation

1. The Developer Cockpit (GUI)

FurryOS_Builder_GUI.py is your visual command center. It manages your 1.1GB payload, 147 custom songs, and HD assets. One click deploys your entire franchise into the build engine.

2. The Master Engine (The Logic)

build_furryos.sh is an elegantly engineered 199-line power-house. It calculates your hardware limits, caps RAM at 75% for safety, and performs a Lighting Manifestation of a custom OS in under 5 minutes.

3. The Mansion Launcher (The Portal)

launch_mansion.sh is the high-performance portal. It boots your unpacked OS folder directly into a 3GB RAM, 4-core VM with smooth mouse support and direct internet access.

4. The Asset Restorer (The Healer)

install_furryos_assets.sh (via the "Asset Installer" shortcut) is the self-healing mechanism. If your mansion's decor ever gets messy or deleted, this script refreshes your ~/FurryOS folder from the system's immutable root without overwriting your custom additions.
