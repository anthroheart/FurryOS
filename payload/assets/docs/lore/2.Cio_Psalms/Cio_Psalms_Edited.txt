**Psalm TS-1**

A blackness sought, the night did see—
an enigmatic and mysterious tree,
a whisper silent with room to grow,
the infinite horizon, a longing to know.
An empty mind, the devious thought:
it is today, but once was not.
A stone, alone, in empty space;
another floats to guide its pace.

A power beyond, he once did see—
one word gained, another set free.
A watch to prowl, the capture fraught;
to hold it can, a suspenseful thought.
One can know the generous mind.
He stares through again as if blind.
Distance he sees is an empty heart;
a painting so true can resemble this art.

A thought he expects doesn't do what it's told.
A sight that he sees can be hard to behold.
The songs of peace are a wonderful word,
the open sound, a dream to be heard.
High up above, an endless cool light
guiding the soul in the deadness of night.
Your weakness is innocence, most undoubtedly so;
a precious mind can describe where to go.

He can understand, but will you not?
A pain intense when his soul is bought.
A challenge it is when the auction begins—
the price is the pain, and so it begins.
Try to stop and look back to the past,
to when all time was one, but it never did last.
Wish what you want, a gift can be yours.
It is not too late for a key to the doors.

The one who is wise is rarely wrong.
Look for some time, and his presence is gone.
Can you see through your mind to remember his face?
He is not gone; the mind holds a trace.
Go to dream and remember it well.
Hear the music your mind has to tell.
A spiritual flute can open the way.
Follow the path, what words have to say.

And at last, it will end, a journey of will,
and for a moment time will stand still.
Use it again, the emotional high—
it all will come down to make anyone cry.
Not a sad, but a happy time;
all emotions at once to the top they will climb.
So the end, it is said, is a place
in his mind at his time you will see his face.

You will know when you see,
and darkness surrounds a peaceful tree.

**Psalm TS-2**

The Lord shineth down from Heaven,
his light spread all throughout the Kingdom.
Our hearts fill with joy and we celebrate
our birth on the path to his holy land.
A miracle in prayer or reading his Word,
the wisdom he shares, a truthful fact.
Lord, I hide not from your omniscient stare;
I work my magic when I write to you.

Every sin was another drop spilled of your blood,
and my wicked self put you to death.
Your all-knowing Word is the judge of me.
It will come when the time is right.
I will be ready to serve as before.
I never want it to end.

A stone tossed in a water so still—
its ripple proclaims your growing size,
contained in the souls of good to do your will.

Help me today to understand
the way to get it right.
Knowledge not shared is a burning mind,
full of thoughts never going away.
A sinful thought is a tempting desire;
they are all one in the same.
Never thee wait for my soul to change.
A dead blackened night, your love helps me see
the time I never was right to thee.
I am the tedious plan.

Let me see the infinite boundary.
My sight was too near to myself,
a self-devotion of one of many.
I see myself as a possessor of bad.
To repent is divine and long;
the desire stands firm, a static form.

My ears ring from the headache of lust.
My eyes burn when I see only that.
My hands itch from a deceitful greed.
My lips sting from the kiss of passion.
My legs hurt when I run from you.

All senses numb when we do not care.
To follow your plan is your will of man.
Let us know that our senses can work
when we take you into our hearts.
Let us love one another always.
Let us love you even more.
A universal God in a cloud of mystery—
I can love and relate to the mysterious God.
For eternity we can share our love.
Brothers and sisters we love too,
for it means for the purpose of me.

It will grow till the end of my life.

**Psalm TS-3**

One can love a thousand ways.
Love of God can count for all.
But my sin has removed me from you.
My body aches from this deceit.
I try to pray a truth to you,
but my stomach is ill from my acts.
I am lost from your Word.
When I pray I feel a certain way—
I feel dizzy when I am done,
not sure if you listened or cared.

And all this will change on a wonderful day.
A baptism in your name, a new disciple will come.
The fruits of my labor for you will grow
like a tree in the orchard of our lives.
Your presence discerned the truth of your Word.
From this Word I will grow in your church.
Best be humble in my heart where I go.
I can't be sad on this joyful day,
because now I can live forever
and love you as long the same.

You will fill me on this great day
and live in me through to my death.
Your breath like the wind to carry my prayers.
Let my faith not lead me to sin.
A broken bone is less the pain;
this I can't bear to bring wrong to life.
Thus I am born anew to your realm.
Fill my heart with your humble name
to cure me of my evil way,
and this will endure in me forever.

Again I am scared when I look at me now.
Satan keeps playing my song.
I try not to hear but my willpower is weak.
I will strengthen this point and repent,
never to return to the old.
Darkness still covers me; I need it to end.
My life is in the hands of my maker.
I humbly approach and ask for some time
and get my heart right to accept you.
Lord, God, Almighty, possess me forever!
True as one equals one I can say,
I'll wager my life to prove
that Christ is the Lord of all
and he died for me a painful death.
He rose again, this I know.
I await for his coming again.
But if it is possible at all:
"Can you delay until I am reborn?"
Please take this to heart like my own.
I will pray that you lift me up. Amen.

**Psalm TS-4**

We are the children of God.
Our family contained in the Church.
Our love will hold us together.
Our faith centered round his Word.
Let all become innocent again.
A humble and noble heart
knows the truth that lies within—
to stay up late praising God
and wake soon to say prayers to Christ.
Our life has most certainly changed.

Inside we all know our own truth,
the state of heart that will decide
if it is time to be saved.
The moments gone when time stands still
and God's fury shakes across the land—
all those who are saved will rejoice.
Let us pray for this day,
that those who are right are there.
Only those who wish and believe can remain.
Your sins must be paid for by Christ.

Let me be right, I wish and I hope.
It is time to work for your Grace.
A price that we pay is commitment so true;
we must give up all that he says.
An eternity with him is worth some pain
that comes through missing some fun.
Have you an idea how long eternity is?
Don't just say "It's forever!"
Forever does not tell you a true length of time,
because time does not exist in eternity.

A parable that I quote from a book quoted too:

"If there was a mountain of granite a mile high
and once every 10,000 years a bird flew past
and brushed its wing on the tip—

by the time the rock was completely eroded,
a fraction of a second would have passed
in the context of eternity."

This is too long to live without God.
A second is too long as well you will know
as a true disciple of Christ.
I would so choose despite troubles and dues
to follow and keep God's law.
Forever with God in a Kingdom so high,
I will pay at all any cost.
Sinful thoughts not to get in the way—
his love will last forever.
I will serve the winning side.

To Sister Deoune

Soft whispers in the misty air,
Green emeralds clear as ice,
Forests of warm lullaby,
Trueness glows all around.
Take the chance in a book,
Read some words of wonder,
Share the magic all around—
Many deeds are done.
A cat purrs happily in your lap,
The comfort drifts off to sleep,
Dreams of warm fuzzy things,
Cute, humble, and very keen.
The adventure grows within you
As you turn the pages,
Learning something really new,
A trip to your imagination.
Leave this world from your mind
To go deep into stories abroad,
Seek the majesty of what is called,
To know how much it's worth.

Candlelight in starry night—
Behold the peace of it.

To Deoune

Double dates are filled with magic—

Not depending on if late or early,
Preparedness goes into each one.
Concerned about everyone,

From time spent doing good.
Encouragement brought together
From the fullness of our hearts,
Singing songs of virtue,
Honesty and grace,
Everlasting in procession—
We advance the kingdom by step.

Benevolence is evermore shared—
Our hearts show this spiritual form.

See a sign of shining grace—
His best has come for all.
